Basic Pooja Steps

  1. Clean and prepare the pooja area.
  2. Place the Ganpati idol respectfully.
  3. Perform the Sthapana and invoke Lord Ganesha.
  4. Offer flowers, Durva and Naivedya.
  5. Perform the Ganpati Aarti.
  6. Distribute Prasad among family members.

Aarti

Ganpati Aarti can be performed according to your family's traditional practice.

Tip: Keep all required pooja items ready before starting the Aarti.

Visarjan Information

Ganpati Visarjan marks the conclusion of the Ganpati celebration. Follow local authority guidelines and designated immersion locations.

Festival Tips

  • Keep the pooja area clean.
  • Prepare pooja materials in advance.
  • Prefer environmentally responsible practices (eco-friendly clay idols).
  • Follow local festival and immersion guidelines.
